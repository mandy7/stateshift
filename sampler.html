How it works

One WhatsApp message — who you are, where you are. Your sachets are blended and sealed the same week. You brew slowly (the card walks you through each pause), then tell us what you felt. Not a star rating — a conversation.

Why now is different

One day StateShift will be a finished thing on a shelf. Right now it is a bench you can pull a chair up to. Founding Tasters drink versions that will never exist again, talk directly with the person who makes them, and quietly shape what the final blends become. Your feedback goes into the same notebook as every breakthrough on this site.

Fair warning

Shelf-stability testing are underway and we suggest to consume the samples within 2 weeks to get the most of aroma and taste ; every box says so plainly. What arrives is hand-made in a batch small enough to sign — the same cups on our own table every morning. If you want polish, wait for the shelf. If you want fingerprints on the craft — yours among them — this is the only moment that offer exists.
