Why this edition exists

This is not our permanent collection. It is our working one.

Lamhe is still on the bench. Sehar is still hunting a bergamot I cannot buy in this country. Every note that comes back changes the next batch, which means you are tasting this coffee before we have finished making it.

What if I don't like it?

If you genuinely don't enjoy it, email me and I will refund you. No forms, no arguments, no return shipping. I would rather know. Almost everything on the Lab page came from someone telling me a cup was plain, or once, memorably, half-cooked.

How do I brew it?

Cup mix into an empty cup, filter bag hooked on the rim, 150ml of hot water poured slowly, three minutes, lift the bag out, stir. A cup and hot water is the entire equipment list. The illustrated card comes in the box, and the full method is here.

How long does it keep?

Drink it inside a month. The sachets are sealed, but aromatics fade faster than coffee does and the aroma is most of the point. Do not save these for a special occasion. The occasion is Tuesday.
