Before you ask
How it works

One WhatsApp message. Who you are, where you are. That is the whole checkout.

Your sachets are blended and sealed the same week they ship. You brew slowly; the card walks you through each pause. Then you tell me what you felt. Not a star rating. A conversation.

Why now is different

One day StateShift will be a finished thing on a shelf. Right now it's a bench you can pull a chair up to.

Founding Tasters drink versions that will never exist again, talk directly to the person who makes them, and quietly shape what the final blends become. Your feedback goes into the same notebook as every breakthrough on this site.

What you're actually buying

Not polish. A batch small enough to sign, weighed on a kitchen scale by one person, on the morning it goes out.

These are the same cups on our own table. The same grind, the same three minutes, the same tenth of a gram. Some of these blends will change after you drink them, partly because of what you tell me.

If you want the finished article, wait for the shelf. If you want your fingerprints on it, this is the only window that exists.
