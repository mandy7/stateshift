Why this exists

This is not about productivity optimisation or biohacking or any of those words that make wellness sound like engineering.

It is about paying attention. To how certain rituals shape attention, calm, energy, and presence throughout a day. To what it feels like when something shifts — and whether that shift can be made deliberate, documented, and eventually shared.

The coffee is the medium. The state is the message. Everything else is observation.
