How it works

Two sachets. One cup. Nothing to wash up.

Coffee grounds want to be steeped and lifted out. Creamer and jaggery want to dissolve. Put them in the same bag and you get a gritty cup, so we keep them apart until the water brings them together.

Sachet A

The filter bag

Freshly ground single-estate coffee and the aromatics. Hangs on the rim of your cup, steeps, comes out.

Sachet B

The cup mix

Creamer, palm jaggery, cocoa where the blend calls for it. Goes into the empty cup first.

Before you ask
Is it tea or coffee?

Coffee. It hangs on your cup like a tea bag because it is a pour-over filter, but inside is freshly ground single-estate coffee. Sehar borrows a little Earl Grey for its citrus. That is the only tea on the shelf.

What if I don't like it?

Tell me and I will refund you, no argument and no form to fill in. I would rather know. Almost everything I have learned came from someone saying a cup was plain, or once, memorably, half-cooked.

When does it arrive?

Boxes are filled by hand and go out the week you order. Anywhere in India, expect it inside a week. You will get a message from me, not a tracking bot.
