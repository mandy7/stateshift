Every coffee changes your day.
Most of us never choose how.

These four were not designed around flavour. They were designed around a state of mind. Morning clarity. Midday composure. Evening reflection. And the hour that belongs to nobody else.

Coffee happens to be the vehicle.

Every other coffee is sorted by how it was roasted. Light, medium, dark. Ours are sorted by when you need them.

SEHAR

dawn Foggy → Clear

Bergamot, orange peel, milk chocolate, lemon candy.

On the noseBergamot. Crisp citrus that cuts through morning fog.
In the cupOrange peel. Sweet, sunlit citrus that lingers through the finish.
The finishMilk chocolate and lemon candy. Gentle sweetness against a lively acidity.
BodyLight. Nothing heavy on an empty stomach.
The moment it was made for

The day has not decided what it will be yet. Neither have you. Sehar is for that window, before the noise and before the phone.

the citrus is ours. oranges, a peeler, an oven, one very fragrant weekend

RUKH

Cardamom, dark chocolate, toasted almond, ripe berries.

On the noseCardamom. It arrives before the coffee does.
In the cupDark chocolate and toasted almonds, in layers.

How it works

Two sachets. One cup. Nothing to wash up.

Coffee grounds want to be steeped and lifted out. Creamer and jaggery want to dissolve. Put them in the same bag and you get a gritty cup, so we keep them apart until the water brings them together. It looks like extra packaging. It is the reason the cup is clean.

Sachet A

The filter bag

Freshly ground single-estate coffee and the aromatics. Hangs on the rim of your cup, steeps, comes out.

Sachet B

The cup mix

Creamer, palm jaggery, cocoa where the blend calls for it. Goes into the empty cup first.

Cup mix in · filter bag on
150ml hot water · three minutes
lift it out · stir · drink
Is it tea or coffee?

Coffee. It hangs on your cup like a tea bag because it is a pour-over filter, but inside is freshly ground single-estate coffee. Sehar borrows a little Earl Grey for its citrus, and that is the only tea on the shelf.

Why three minutes?

Because that is how long the aroma takes to arrive, and because it is long enough to be a pause and short enough that you will actually take it. There is nothing to grind, nothing to weigh and nothing to wash up. The cup gives your hands something to hold while you arrive.
