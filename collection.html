Every coffee changes your day.
Most of us never choose how.

Sehar, Rukh, Kisse and Lamhe were not built around flavour. Each one was built for a particular hour, and for the way that hour tends to slip past unnoticed.

Every other coffee is sorted by how it was roasted. Light, medium, dark. Ours are sorted by when you need them.

How it works

Two sachets. One cup. Nothing to wash up.

Coffee grounds want to be steeped and lifted out. Creamer and jaggery want to dissolve. Put them in the same bag and you get a gritty cup, so we keep them apart until the water brings them together.

Sachet A

The filter bag

Freshly ground single-estate coffee and the aromatics. Hangs on the rim of your cup, steeps, comes out.

Sachet B

The cup mix

Creamer, palm jaggery, cocoa where the blend calls for it. Goes into the empty cup first.

Cup mix in · filter bag on
150ml hot water · three minutes
lift it out · stir · drink
Why three minutes?

Because that is how long the aroma takes to arrive. Pull the bag out at ninety seconds and you get coffee. Leave it the full three and you get the thing we built. It is also long enough to be a pause and short enough that you will actually take it.

Do I need a machine, a grinder, anything?

A cup and hot water. That is the entire equipment list. Everything that needed grinding was ground by hand, that morning, before it was sealed.

Can I make it with milk?

Water first, always. Milk mutes the aroma, and the aroma is most of the point. If you want it softer, add a splash of hot milk after the filter bag comes out.
